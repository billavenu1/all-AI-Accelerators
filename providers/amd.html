Merchant GPU

AMD: Helios (MI455X + EPYC Venice)

AMD's first rack-scale system: 72 MI455X GPUs and 18 EPYC Venice CPUs, wired together with open standards instead of proprietary links. Launched in July 2026, with volume shipments from Q3.

Rack infrastructure

Power & liquid cooling

Meta's Open Rack Wide design, shared through the Open Compute Project

Why it's special: Helios isn't built on a proprietary rack. It uses Open Rack Wide, a double-width design Meta contributed to the Open Compute Project in 2025. The extra width leaves room for big liquid manifolds and power busbars, and any OCP-compatible data centre can take it.

Key numbers

1.2 mwide, 44 OU tallPRESS
~385 L/mincoolant flowPRESS
~5,000 lbpre-launch weight estimatePRESS

What it does here

  • A liquid-cooled 50 V busbar runs power down the rack.
  • Trays plug into coolant and power with blind-mate connectors at the back: slide in, and they connect.
  • Schneider Electric and AMD publish a reference design for sites up to 246 kW per rack.

Scale-up network

Scale-up switch trays

×6 trays · 2 Broadcom Tomahawk 6 chips each PRESS

Why it's special: instead of a proprietary fabric like NVLink, Helios runs the open UALink protocol over standard Ethernet switch chips ("UALink over Ethernet"). Each GPU spreads its links across 12 separate switch planes, so if one switch fails, a GPU loses only 1/12 of its bandwidth instead of dropping out.

Key numbers

3.6 TB/sper GPU (36 × 400 Gb/s links)
260 TB/sacross the rackPRESS
12switch chips, 102.4 Tb/s eachPRESS
AES-256encryption at line ratePRESS

What it does here

  • Lets all 72 GPUs share memory and split one model as a single system, in one switch hop.
  • Runs the open-source SONiC switch OS with AMD's Fabric Manager PRESS.
  • HPE's version uses Juniper switches, showing the vendor choice an open fabric allows.

Host CPU

EPYC "Venice"

×18 per rack · 6th-gen EPYC · Zen 6 on TSMC 2nm

Why it's special: AMD designs the CPU, the GPU, the NICs and the DPU in this rack, the only merchant chip vendor besides Nvidia to do so. Venice is also the first server CPU with PCIe Gen6, and AMD claims over 70% more performance and efficiency than the previous Turin generation.

Key numbers (Venice family)

96 / 256max cores: Zen 6 / dense Zen 6cPRESS
1.6 TB/smemory bandwidth, 16 channelsPRESS
5 GHzpeak clock (Zen 6)PRESS
70%+gain vs Turin (AMD claim)

What it does here

  • Runs the tray's OS and feeds four GPUs; Helios reportedly uses a 96-core part PRESS.
  • Talks to each GPU over a 256 GB/s Infinity Fabric link PRESS, far less than Nvidia's 1.8 TB/s CPU–GPU link.
  • PCIe Gen6 and CXL 3.1 for NICs, storage and memory expansion.

Scale-out: Ultra Ethernet

Outside the rack · any vendor's Ethernet switches

Why it's special: AMD doesn't sell its own cluster switches. Helios racks connect over standard Ethernet tuned for AI by the Ultra Ethernet Consortium, which AMD helped found. Customers can use switches from Broadcom, Cisco, Arista, Juniper or others, where Nvidia steers buyers to its own Spectrum-X or InfiniBand.

Concept

Rack power & liquid cooling

A modern AI rack draws as much power as a hundred or more homes, and almost all of it becomes heat, far more than fans can remove.

Direct liquid cooling pumps coolant through plates on each chip. A busbar is a thick metal rail that carries power down the rack instead of hundreds of cables.

Analogy: a car engine. Air cooling works for a lawnmower; a racing engine needs a radiator.
Concept

What is a compute tray?

A tray is a flat drawer that slides into the rack, holding a few accelerators, their CPU and network chips on one board.

Racks are built from identical trays, so a broken one can be swapped like a battery.

Analogy: books on a shelf. The rack is the shelf, each tray is a book, and you can pull one out without moving the rest.
Concept

What is a scale-up network?

Big models are split across many GPUs that must swap partial results constantly. A scale-up network links GPUs inside one rack with very high bandwidth and low delay so they act like one big GPU.

UALink is an open industry standard for this, backed by AMD, Intel, Google, Microsoft, Meta and others, as an alternative to Nvidia's NVLink.

Analogy: people in one open-plan room who can just talk, versus emailing another office (scale-out).
Concept

What is a GPU?

A GPU runs thousands of simple calculations in parallel. AI is mostly multiplying huge grids of numbers, which is exactly that kind of work.

Next to it sits HBM, stacked memory that holds the model. How fast the GPU reads HBM often limits speed more than the maths does.

Analogy: a CPU is a few expert chefs; a GPU is a thousand line cooks each chopping one vegetable at the same time.
Concept

What is a host CPU?

Accelerators can't run an operating system. A host CPU boots the machine, loads data, launches work onto the GPUs and handles everything that isn't matrix maths, including the tool calls and code execution agents rely on.

Analogy: the head chef who reads the orders and hands tasks to the line cooks.
Concept

What is an AI NIC?

A network interface card connects a computer to the network. An AI NIC is built for GPU clusters: huge bandwidth per GPU, hardware congestion control, and the ability to move data straight into GPU memory (RDMA) without involving the CPU.

Analogy: a dedicated loading dock for each warehouse aisle, instead of one front door for the whole building.
Concept

What is a DPU?

A data processing unit is a network card with its own CPU cores and memory. It takes over the data centre's "plumbing": networking, storage access, encryption, firewalls and keeping customers isolated.

P4 is a language for programming how network hardware handles packets, so a P4-based DPU can learn new protocols through software.

Analogy: a building's security desk and mailroom. Residents (the GPUs) never deal with deliveries or visitors themselves.
Concept

What is a scale-out network?

Scale-out networks connect racks to each other so thousands of racks can work on one model.

Ultra Ethernet is a new standard from the Ultra Ethernet Consortium that adds AI-friendly features (smarter congestion control, packet spraying, faster recovery) to ordinary Ethernet.

Analogy: the motorway between cities, while scale-up is the streets inside one city.

Fabric & cache dies (FCDs)

2 chiplets on TSMC 3nm · 4 XCDs each

Why it's special: these two dies hold a combined 192 MB of L2 cache, a very large on-chip buffer between the compute chiplets and HBM. They link all eight XCDs fast enough that software sees one GPU with one pool of memory, not eight small GPUs.

Concept

What is an XCD (and a chiplet)?

A chiplet is a small die that is combined with others in one package to make a bigger chip. An XCD (accelerator complex die) is AMD's compute chiplet, full of compute units: AMD's equivalent of Nvidia's SMs.

Small dies are cheaper because one defect ruins less silicon.

Analogy: building a house from prefabricated rooms instead of carving it from one giant block.
Concept

What is a fabric & cache die?

When a GPU is made of chiplets, something has to connect them. A fabric die is the wiring that lets every compute chiplet reach every memory stack.

AMD also puts the L2 cache there: fast on-chip memory that keeps recently used data close to the compute units.

Analogy: a central station with a big waiting hall. Every line passes through it, and travellers wait close to their platforms.
Concept

What is HBM?

High-bandwidth memory stacks DRAM chips on top of each other and places them right beside the processor, connected by thousands of tiny wires.

It is much faster than normal memory, but expensive and limited in size, so it is often the real bottleneck for AI.

Analogy: a multi-storey car park next to the stadium, with a hundred exits instead of one.
Concept

What is an I/O die?

An I/O die holds the circuits that send signals off the chip: to other GPUs, the CPU and the network. These analog circuits barely benefit from newer, costlier manufacturing processes, so they go on a separate chiplet.

Analogy: a factory's loading bay. It doesn't need the fanciest machinery, just lots of doors.
Concept

What is a CPU host link?

The host link connects the GPU to the CPU that controls it. Infinity Fabric is AMD's family of links between its own chips.

A faster link lets the GPU use the CPU's memory as overflow; a slower one means the GPU leans on its own HBM.

Analogy: the corridor between the head chef's office and the kitchen.
