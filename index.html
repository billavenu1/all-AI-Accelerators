Three camps build AI chips. Each attacks a different limit.

Merchant GPUs sell to anyone

General-purpose parallel compute with large stacks of HBM4 memory, from Nvidia and AMD.

Hyperscaler ASICs stay in one cloud

Custom chips tuned for one company's workloads. You rent them, never buy them.

Specialists attack the memory wall

Compute beside the memory, a whole wafer as one chip, or SRAM only.

Questions

The terms you need before opening a rack.

Read the primer

How do training and inference differ?

Training wants raw compute and very large clusters. Inference wants memory bandwidth and a low cost per token.

What do FP4 and FP8 petaflops measure?

Peak compute at 4-bit or 8-bit floating point. Lower precision gives bigger numbers, so only compare like with like.

How is HBM different from SRAM?

HBM is stacked memory beside the chip: large, but limited in bandwidth. SRAM sits on the chip itself: tiny and extremely fast.

What are scale-up and scale-out?

Scale-up links the chips inside a rack into one system. Scale-out links racks into a cluster over Ethernet or InfiniBand.

Which vendor figures can I compare?

Fewer than you would hope. Per-chip, per-rack and per-pod figures get mixed freely in vendor material.
